# Suwenzo SEO Keyword Map (Source of Truth)

## Positioning
Suwenzo is positioned strictly as "clinic software" for private outpatient clinics and medical practices in Sri Lanka, not "hospital management software" (HMS), laboratory information management systems (LIMS), or retail pharmacy inventory software.

Key Differentiators in Copy:
- Prescription-to-label-to-stock flow for clinics with an in-house dispensary.
- Complete patient records with visit history and allergy flags.
- Automated WhatsApp follow-up reminders with patient consent captured in one tap.
- Full onboarding set up with the clinic within 48 hours.
