Target Audience: Private clinics and medical practices in Sri Lanka with an in-house dispensary · 3 months free for founding clinics.
Founder Origin: Built in Colombo by a founder who sat in these clinics as a patient.

The Problem (ProblemSection.tsx)

Headline: Where a paper-run clinic quietly loses time and money
Subtitle: Three things repeat every evening session.

Problem 01

Written twice, by two people

The doctor writes the prescription. The dispenser reads it, re-enters it, and hand-writes a label for every packet. Same information twice, with a queue watching.

Cost: Slower queues and more chances for a misread dose.
Problem 02

Prescribing without seeing stock

The doctor can't see what's on the shelf. When a medicine is out, the patient gets sent to a pharmacy down the road.

Cost: Dispensary revenue the clinic earned and handed away.
Problem 03

Phone numbers in a drawer

Every patient's number sits in a paper file and never gets used. Nobody follows up when a course of medicine ends.

Cost: Patients who feel better on day 7 and never come back.

Frequently Asked Questions (FaqSection.tsx)

Headline: Questions doctors ask before they book a demo
Subtitle: Clear, honest answers about pricing, clinic hardware, stock setup, and day-to-day dispensary workflow.

1. How much does ClinKare cost?
ClinKare costs LKR 3,500 per month for a clinic where the doctor prescribes, and LKR 7,000 per month for a clinic with an in-house dispensary. Founding clinics get their first 3 months free. Setup and training are included.
2. What does the founding clinic offer include?
The first 10 clinics in Colombo get 3 months free. After that, you continue at your locked price for as long as you stay. Founding clinics also tell us what to build next.
3. Who is ClinKare built for?
ClinKare is built for private clinics and medical practices in Sri Lanka, especially practices with an in-house dispensary where the doctor prescribes and the dispenser hands out the medicine. It fits single-doctor and growing multi-doctor practices.
4. What do I need to buy to get started?
You need a tablet for the doctor's desk, a label printer at the dispensary, and an internet connection. Before you buy anything, we share the list of printers that work with ClinKare so nothing is wasted.
5. How long does setup take, and who does it?
We do it with you. We enter your stock list, connect your printer and train your dispenser, so you start with a working system, not an empty one. Most clinics are ready in 48 hours from the demo.
6. Will my dispenser be able to use it?
Yes. The dispenser's screen shows the prescription the moment you confirm it, so there's nothing to read and nothing to re-type. We train your dispenser during setup and stay on WhatsApp for the first weeks.
7. Does ClinKare work without internet?
ClinKare needs an internet connection. A standard mobile hotspot or 4G clinic router is enough. If your connection temporarily drops, prescriptions are safely queued and resume the moment signal reconnects.
