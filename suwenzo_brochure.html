Suwenzo Healthcare Platform

Cloud-Native SaaS Intelligence for Integrated Clinic, Pharmacy & Lab Networks

1. Executive Overview

Suwenzo is a modern, cloud-native Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) platform engineered to connect consultation rooms, pharmacy counters, and a fully-fledged laboratory system in real time. Designed to be accessed and operated from anywhere on any device or web browser, Suwenzo eliminates operational bottlenecks and provides complete visibility across multi-branch healthcare ecosystems.

By seamlessly orchestrating patient flow, prescription fulfillment, and lab requisitions, Suwenzo enables healthcare enterprises to deliver faster, safer, and higher-quality care at scale.

2. Core System Modules & Features

Smart Clinic & Consultation Engine

  • Multi-Input Prescription Workflows: Supports quick-tap buttons for common medications and instant search, ensuring frictionless prescribing for doctors.
  • Live Inventory Visibility: Real-time stock matching alerts doctors to medicine availability during consultations.
  • Dedicated Printing Features: Explicitly supports patient ID card issuing, queue number printing, and prescription printing directly from the system.
  • Unified Electronic Medical Records (EMR): Instant access to complete patient medical histories and clinical notes.

High-Speed Pharmacy & Inventory

  • Smart Drug Swapping: Ability to swap drugs seamlessly based on active real-time inventory availability.
  • Dosage & Stock Balancing: In-built features that allow for precise dosage changes, alongside separate, dedicated methods for drug balancing across shelves.
  • Automated Configurable Expiry Alerts: System alerts for upcoming drug expiries that are fully configurable, such as sending notifications at 3 months instead of 30 days.
  • Drug Consumption Tracking: Tracks and displays drug consumption so users can estimate their remaining drug balances by the end of the month.

Connected Laboratory System

  • Fully-Fledged Laboratory System: A complete diagnostic module tightly integrated into the primary clinical workflow.
  • Analyzer Device Interfacing: Capability to integrate directly with analyzers (machines that analyze tests) to generate direct reports.
  • Direct Order Requisition Sync: Laboratory test orders created by doctors sync directly to the lab queue without manual hand-offs.

Patient Engagement

  • Automated Notifications: Keep patients engaged with automated appointment reminders and treatment follow-ups (configurable for email, WhatsApp, and SMS).
